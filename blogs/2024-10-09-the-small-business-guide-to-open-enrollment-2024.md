Open Enrollment runs from November 1 to December 15 , with some states starting as early as October 15, and some states extending the period through January 31. During this time, you can visit the federal health exchange and find an individual plan. No matter which plan you select, coverage for plans selected during this time begin January 1, 2024 and end December 31, 2024.
